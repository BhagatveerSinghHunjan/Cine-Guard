Why this project fits the Parallel track:

Screenplay risk is fundamentally an outside-the-document problem — the
script says "rooftop helicopter at night" but the answer lives with
permit offices, aviation rules, and location policies. CineGuard's
research planner turns every extracted dependency into bounded Parallel

on every source, reasons over that evidence (never inventing citations),
and records empty results as unknowns rather than facts. Parallel is not
a logo on this project; without live external research the core promise
— "can we realistically shoot this?" — cannot be answered.
